title = "Parallelizing Sequential Computation"

This should be an intimate workshop about the parallelization of seemingly sequential computation, including in:

- RNNs
- MCMC
- Diffusion models
- Differential Equations
- Reinforcement Learning
- Looped transformers
- And more!

We hope this workshop can serve as an opportunity to meet face-to-face with researchers from different corners of this community, who nonetheless use similar tools to solve similar problems.

**This workshop is in-person only and by invitation only.**
